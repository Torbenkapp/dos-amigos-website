Our Story: From a Trailer to Your Town

It all started with a newly bought trailer that showed no signs of what it would become. As two engineering students, we had a big dream and the skills to make it reality.

The first frame stands! Everything was measured and calculated by us engineering students. The frame is unboltable and the whole house can be placed off the trailer - true modular engineering.

Our first day of sales in the city center of Horsens! After countless hours of hard work, the moment finally arrived. The feeling of serving our first burrito was unforgettable!
